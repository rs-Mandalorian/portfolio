Cox CommunicationsPlatform modernization

Give localized content
a better foundation.

I led the migration of approximately 24,000 localized pages from Yext to Adobe Experience Manager, bringing content, data, and publishing requirements into a reusable structure.

≈$1Min annual vendor cost eliminated

What I owned

The product decisions that make reuse possible.

Reuse depended on making the content and data work in the new platform. I defined field mappings and schema requirements, cleansed migration data, and recreated personalization rules for the new experience. I collaborated on the content models, owned the project's content fragments, and managed the library's scope, approvals, supported features, and change governance.

4content models
35content fragments
66reusable experience fragments
12existing experience fragments reused
More about the work +

I created a SQL database to ingest and normalize Yext Knowledge Graph data. That gave the team a repeatable source for AEM content structures.

I co-defined the AEM structures with technical partners, wrote user stories and acceptance criteria, and coordinated phased releases designed to protect revenue continuity.

As visualization, documentation, and delivery workflows were standardized using Fiogma and FigJam, the projected delivery timeline fell from approximately two years to one.
