I created a SQL database to ingest and normalize Yext Knowledge Graph data. That gave the team a repeatable source for AEM content structures.

I co-defined the AEM structures with technical partners, wrote user stories and acceptance criteria, and coordinated phased releases designed to protect revenue continuity. The counts describe this project's library.

As visualization, documentation, and delivery workflows were standardized, the projected delivery timeline fell from approximately two years to one.

02 / How I work

Understand the problem.
Make the decisions clear.
Keep improving.

I've worked across customer-facing products, content platforms, and ecommerce. I bring that experience to the details that determine whether a platform works for customers and the people running it.

01

Start with the customer and the work.

Use customer conversations, search behavior, analytics, and operational feedback to clarify what needs to change.

02

Make the requirements usable.

Translate the problem into priorities, product rules, data requirements, and acceptance criteria the team can build against.

03

Plan for the people operating it.

Consider content reuse, publishing workflows, governance, and support needs alongside the customer experience.

04

Measure, learn, and adjust.

Use release feedback and product performance to decide what to improve next.
